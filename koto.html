KOTO by Toyota

Car care, without the friction

I designed one mobile experience for discovering services, booking care, and securely sharing vehicle access.

The problem

Vehicle ownership was split across disconnected moments

Owners searched for services without clear prices, coordinated appointments through calls and emails, then managed physical keys separately.

  1. 1
    Opaque pricing

    Hidden fees made it difficult to commit with confidence.

  2. 2
    Scheduling friction

    Booking stretched across calls and confirmations.

  3. 3
    Awkward handoffs

    Physical key exchange complicated service and shared access.

The opportunity was not another booking tool. It was one continuous relationship between the owner, the car, and Toyota.

Research insight

Trust depends on clarity before convenience

Interviews with 20 vehicle owners, service-provider conversations, behavioral analysis, and competitive review revealed three conditions for digital adoption.

01

Show the full cost

Transparent pricing removes the fear of surprise charges.

02

Confirm every handoff

Availability, status, and reminders make booking predictable.

03

Explain access

Visible permissions help digital keys feel secure.

Design decision

Unify the journey around the vehicle

One account. One car. One continuous service relationship.

Evidence
Owners understood tasks better when services, appointments, and access were grounded in their vehicle.
Decision
Make the vehicle the shared context for shopping, scheduling, tracking, and key permissions.
Tradeoff
Accept a more connected system architecture to remove repeated setup and fragmented handoffs.

Final design

Two product pillars, designed as one experience

The visual system keeps high-value actions clear while the vehicle profile carries context across every task.

Service marketplace

Discover and book with confidence

Owners browse relevant services, see pricing before committing, choose an available time, and receive a clear confirmation.

  • Vehicle-aware recommendations
  • Transparent price and availability
  • Fewer steps from discovery to booking
Appointment management

Keep the next step visible

Upcoming service, appointment details, and live status remain easy to find, reducing follow-up calls.

  • Clear appointment state
  • Timely preparation guidance
  • Progress attached to the vehicle
Digital key

Make access secure and shareable

Owners can add a vehicle, understand key status, and share access for family, valet, or service without exchanging a physical key.

  • Explicit permission and vehicle context
  • Simple add-car setup
  • Contactless service handoff

Iteration

Structure came before surface

Sketches and low-fidelity flows tested how much information owners needed at each decision before visual styling entered the process.

The integrated experience improved service conversion and confidence in connected vehicle access.

What I learned

In an opaque category, clarity is not a finishing touch—it is the product value. Price, status, and permission design made convenience believable.

What I would test next

Mileage-based maintenance recommendations, stronger digital-key recovery, and accessibility in outdoor and gloved-use contexts.

Design the relationship, not just the transaction.
