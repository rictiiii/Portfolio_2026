What are sales taxes?

Most states or localities impose a sales tax on goods and services. Always check with your local tax authority to be sure.

Why do we need this information?

Our team will build your menu with the information you provide, so that Shift4 Dine automatically calculates sales tax. Maintaining accurate taxes are the business owners responsibility.

Exclusive vs. Inclusive

Exclusive taxes will be added to the item subtotal, while inclusive taxes are included in the price of the item.
