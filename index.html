About

I’m a Senior Product Designer with 10+ years of experience turning complex operational systems into clear, scalable products. At Shift4, I lead end-to-end design for merchant onboarding, payments, kiosk, and AI-assisted experiences used by 20,000+ businesses.

My strongest work sits at the intersection of systems thinking, product strategy, and hands-on execution. I bring clarity to ambiguous problems, simplify data-rich and regulated workflows, and partner closely with product and engineering to ship across web, mobile, desktop, and touch-screen platforms.

I’ve delivered outcomes including 60% higher completion, 40% fewer escalations, and 50% faster design-to-development handoffs while building design systems that support 5+ product squads. I’m especially interested in AI-native products—using rapid prototyping and code to explore how intelligent systems can become more useful, trustworthy, and actionable.
